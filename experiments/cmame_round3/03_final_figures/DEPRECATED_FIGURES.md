plate/roof/M3 displacement rows came from reconstructed drivers rather than
the recovered manuscript boundary-value problems:

- `cmame_additional/03_scaling/figures/Figure_C1--C3.*`
- `cmame_additional/04_beta_sensitivity/figures/Figure_B1--B3.*`

- Round-3 `Figure_E_beta_sensitivity`;

three disputed benchmark drivers and were rerun after the Round-2 production
optimization.  Curved and boundary spectral figures remain qualified
Appendix diagnostics rather than main benchmark evidence.
